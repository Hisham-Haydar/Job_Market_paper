## 6.1.1 EOp Measurement as a

Multidimensional Problem A direct application of the sequential Lorenz quasi-ordering to this setting is not appropriate, and it is interesting to see why. Of course, effort can be seen as analytically similar to needs: that is, at the margin, the more effort one makes, the more income one deserves, although this statement has limitations. (We may wish not to reward effort excessively, for reasons discussed in section 3.)
