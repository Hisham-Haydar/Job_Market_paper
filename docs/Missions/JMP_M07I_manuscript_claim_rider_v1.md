## 1. WHAT THIS RIDER CHANGES, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

The three identity documents were written before a specification comparison existed. They speak of "the baseline," "the France prototype," and "the opportunity share" as if one specification and one magnitude were in prospect. Two specifications now exist on the same sample and the same geometry, and the deputy's s10 instruction fixes their manuscript roles: **LOC4 is the preferred structural specification; the corrected baseline is the nested reference restriction, reported as such.** This rider therefore does three things and no more. It installs the specification vocabulary into the claims layer. It converts every level and share claim from a promised magnitude into a banded, specification-attributed, profile/MC-pending template. And it carries the specification-limits disclosure in full so that the limits travel with the claims rather than trailing them in an appendix.

## 2. THE BINDING CLAIM-LAYER SETTLEMENT

### 2.1 The interpretation of record (verbatim, not to be paraphrased anywhere in the manuscript)

> "a better-supported structural specification empirically, not a mechanical correction of an invalid baseline estimator"

This sentence is the governing interpretation of the LOC4 disposition. It carries two prohibitions with it, both of which bind manuscript text:

- The manuscript may **not** say or imply that the corrected baseline was invalid, mis-estimated, broken, or repaired. The proposal density is permitted to differ from the structural target density; asymmetry between them is not itself a defect of the baseline likelihood.
- The manuscript may **not** rest the specification preference on that asymmetry. The preference rests on nesting plus rejection of the zero restrictions plus material downstream consequence, in that order.

### 2.2 The two specification roles

| role | manuscript name | permitted description |
|---|---|---|
| preferred structural specification | the preferred specification (occupation-specific wage-location shifts) | strict three-coordinate nested extension; restrictions rejected on the conventional reference; single optimum; clean curvature; extension coefficients interior |
| nested reference restriction | the reference restriction | the preferred specification's own zero restriction on the three occupation shifts; reproduces the reference objective **bitwise** and the welfare path **bitwise** at the restriction; **not** a stationary point of the extension |

**The reference restriction is reported, not withdrawn, and not called a robustness check.** The prior prototype closure is not withdrawn by this rider.

### 2.3 What is claimable now, without further gating

Only these:

1. Component **signs** are stable (access and ability contributions positive, preference contribution negative) on both specifications and on every accepted replicate.
2. Opportunity-related variation is **non-negligible** — the sign survives the specification change.
3. The **nesting fact**, which is algebraic rather than asymptotic, and the fact that the restriction is not stationary in the extension.
4. That the specification change is **material** for the headline welfare objects — stated as a finding about specification sensitivity, without freezing either arm's magnitude.

Everything else in §3–§5 below is templated and pending.

## 3. BANDED LANGUAGE TEMPLATES — W1 LEVELS AND SHARES

**Rule.** No W1 level, Gini, or normalised opportunity share reaches print as a point value. Each appears as an interval with a named source of the band, and each carries the pending condition in the same sentence — not in a footnote.

**Template L-1 — preferred-specification W1 mean.**

> Under the preferred specification, mean money-metric well-being is `{W1_MEAN_PREF} ± {E_T_PREF}`, where the band is the Monte-Carlo band of the preferred arm. This level is reported conditionally: it remains sensitive to a single near-boundary wage-curvature coordinate at a magnitude comparable to the materiality thresholds themselves, and it is not frozen pending the Tier-2 boundary analysis and a precision battery on the preferred arm.

**Template L-2 — preferred-specification W1 Gini.**

> Inequality in money-metric well-being under the preferred specification is `{W1_GINI_PREF} ± {…}`, reported under the same pending condition as the level.

**Template L-3 — the normalised opportunity share.**

> The normalised opportunity contribution is `{S_OPP_PREF}` under the preferred specification and `{S_OPP_REF}` under the reference restriction. The share is a property of the specification, not of the data: the two values differ by roughly a factor of four, and no specification-free magnitude for the opportunity share is available on the present evidence.

**Template L-4 — the specification difference itself (the one comparison that is certified).**

> Allowing occupation-specific wage location changes the headline welfare objects materially: the mean, the Gini, and the normalised opportunity contribution each move by more than their certified difference bands, and the finding is stable across the full replicate family and across every scenario of the local sensitivity battery.

**Template L-5 — the median. Mandatory wherever a central tendency is discussed.**

> The median is uncertified in both specifications, at the level and at the difference, and carries no quantitative claim in this paper.

**Prohibited in all four templates:** "approximately," "about," "roughly" attached to a W1 level or share as a substitute for a band; any share stated once and reused without its specification attribution; any band borrowed across arms (a difference band is never an arm's own band).

## 4. THE ORDERING CLAIM — `PENDING_PROFILE_CONFIRMATION`, BOTH OUTCOMES TEMPLATED

**Status.** The ordering *access > ability > preference* under the preferred specification is **`PENDING_PROFILE_CONFIRMATION`**. It is not asserted in this rider. Both outcomes are templated below; exactly one is installed when the profile/MC confirmation returns, and the other is discarded.

**Template O-A — if the ordering is confirmed on the profile-confirmed run.**

> Under the preferred specification, the access contribution exceeds the ability contribution, which exceeds the preference contribution. Under the nested reference restriction the ordering of the first two is reversed. The ordering is therefore a property of the specification and is reported as such; it is not presented as a property of the data. The reversal arises because the ability contribution falls further than the access contribution, and not because opportunity content is transferred between them: both contributions fall.

**Template O-B — if the ordering is not confirmed, or is confirmed only on part of the profile.**

> The relative ranking of the access and ability contributions is not established at the precision available here. What is established is that the ranking obtained under the nested reference restriction does not survive the specification change, and that the two contributions are not separately identified with sufficient precision to support a ranking claim under the preferred specification. The paper therefore reports the two contributions without ranking them, and the sign claims and the aggregate opportunity claim stand independently of the ranking.

**Binding in both branches.** The pre-registered ordering claim was the *specific* reference-restriction ordering. It is not re-pointed at the preferred specification by substituting the reversed ordering into the same "stable ordering" sentence. It is recorded as a **specification-driven reversal**, with the pre-registered wording shown as superseded.

## 5. PROHIBITED FORMULATIONS (claims layer, standing)

The manuscript may not state, in any section, figure caption, table note, abstract, or slide derived from these documents:

1. That the ordering of components is a property of the data rather than of a named specification.
2. `s_opp ≈ 0.101` — or any restatement of the reference-restriction opportunity share — as a specification-robust, specification-free, or headline magnitude. Where it appears at all it appears **only** as one member of the contrast pair in Template L-3.
3. That results are robust to allowing occupation-specific wage location. They are not; that is the finding.
4. Any claim of quantitative robustness across W1 / W4 / W6, in either direction. The secondary comparator triggers on a different scenario set, moves the mean in the opposite direction under the joint perturbation, and leaves uncertified precisely the inequality objects such a claim would need.
5. Any causal, transfer, or absorption language for `δ_occ`: not a causal occupational wage premium; not a quantity of opportunity content moved from one component to another; not an absorption of preference heterogeneity into opportunity. Both components fall. This is a model-conditional reallocation and welfare-distribution change.
6. That any baseline-versus-preferred coefficient movement is a statistically significant difference.
7. Any median claim, level or difference, any measure, either specification.
8. Any subgroup welfare quantity — level, Gini, share, or component — on either specification.
9. Any interpretation of the uncertified differences beyond "uncertified": not "small," not "probably immaterial," not "directionally suggestive."
10. A specification decision inferred from LR / AIC / BIC alone. These are conventional references at a boundary-adjacent optimum in both arms, and two leisure-curvature coordinates are upper-bound-active on both sides.

## 8. COHERENCE CHECK AGAINST THE ABSTRACT'S CURRENT FRAMING — INCOHERENCES NAMED

Named, not designed around. Each is a finding; none is silently repaired by the replacement text above.

**C-1 · Identity layer versus claims layer, unresolved in the attached copies.**
The attached abstract says *couples choose among latent job packages*; the intro skeleton says *household/couples sample* and *each spouse faces sampled latent job packages*. The binding application is the single-adult P2a sample. Project memory records the identity documents as corrected to v2 through Stage D. **The copies supplied to this mission are pre-Stage-D.** This rider does not correct them — identity is outside its scope — and it writes `{IDENTITY_v2}` rather than a sample-unit noun. **Finding:** either the wrong document versions were attached to this mission, or the Stage-D corrections were not propagated to these three files. This must be resolved before any replacement text above is executed, because several replacements sit adjacent to the defective clauses. **Not resolvable by this rider.**

**C-2 · The abstract's central object is the least robust quantity in the packet.**
The abstract nominates *the share of welfare inequality attributable to unequal opportunities* as the paper's central object. That share is the one headline that moves by roughly a factor of four across the two specifications and fails its own level gate on the reference arm. **This is the sharpest incoherence in the set.** A-2 repairs the sentence; it does not repair the underlying tension, which is that the paper's stated central object cannot currently be delivered as a number. **Named for the record:** the paper's claim to a *quantified* opportunity share is, at present, a claim about a specification-conditional pair, not a magnitude.

**C-3 · Two-component framing versus three reported components.**
Abstract and concept note §4 speak of an opportunity component and a preference component; the intro skeleton §5 specifies a *two-factor* Shapley-Shorrocks decomposition. The executed decomposition reports three components — access, ability, preference. Per the project's standing structure, the headline split is opportunity versus preferences and the access/ability split is a nested supporting analysis, which reconciles the framing **only if the manuscript states the nesting explicitly**. C-1 and I-2 install that statement. **Residual incoherence:** the ordering claim that is `PENDING_PROFILE_CONFIRMATION` lives entirely inside the *nested* layer, while the specification-sensitive share lives in the *headline* layer. These have been treated as one finding in the packet. They are not one finding, and the manuscript should not present them as one.

**C-4 · "Disciplined measurement rather than settled magnitudes" is now both truer and weaker than intended.**
The abstract's closing hedge was written as a posture. It is now a description of a specific, documented blocking condition. The abstract document's own editorial note recommends removing exactly this tentativeness. **Finding:** that editorial recommendation is now in direct conflict with the claims layer and is withdrawn at §7.1.

**C-5 · Governance label conflict — LOC4 "preferred" versus the standing interim instruction.**
The rulings record fixes the standing status as `LOC4_MATERIAL_TIER2_TRIGGER_PREFERRED_SPEC_PENDING` and instructs: *"Do not label LOC4 preferred or restore the baseline ordering as robust."* The s10 instruction under which this rider is issued directs that LOC4 **is** the preferred structural specification. The independent review's own formula reconciles these substantively — *specification preference can be LOC4 now; final level magnitudes remain conditional* — and this rider is drafted to satisfy both readings simultaneously: it labels the specification preferred and freezes no magnitude. **But the reconciliation is substantive, not formal.** The earlier interim instruction is not self-superseding. **Finding:** the rulings document requires an explicit supersession entry recording that the s10 instruction replaces the interim labelling prohibition, before the word "preferred" enters manuscript text. **Not within this rider's authority.**

**C-6 · "Prototype" as a status word is now ambiguous.**
The intro skeleton uses *prototype* to mean "narrow first empirical pass." The governance record uses `M08_BASELINE_PROTOTYPE_LIMITED_MC_PRECISION` to name a closure state attached to the *reference* arm. After the specification change, "the prototype" no longer picks out a unique referent. I-3 removes the word from that paragraph. **Finding:** the term should be retired from manuscript-facing text or bound to one meaning by an explicit definition.

**C-7 · The intro's postponement structure inverts the result.**
The skeleton postpones robustness until after the baseline result is established. Under the s10 settlement the specification comparison *is* a main result, and the postponed-robustness framing would present the paper's most consequential finding as an afterthought. I-4 corrects the roadmap sentence; **the residual finding is structural** — the results section's ordering, which this rider does not have authority to restructure.
