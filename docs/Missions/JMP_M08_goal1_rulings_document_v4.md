DEPUTY RULING — WELFARE-MEASURE DISPOSITION, 8x EXTENSION,
AND RUM INTERPRETATION

1. W2

Do not alter W2 to a high quantile, smooth maximum or soft maximum.

The characterised W2 requires the maximum pay over the actual ability
set after a common income shift. The current implementation uses a
maximum over sampled disposable-income nodes. Its divergence with draw
count is therefore definitional, not ordinary Monte Carlo noise.

W2 is not operational under the current continuous latent-job support
using the sampled-set maximum.

Remove W2 from:
- the M08 source decomposition;
- the S-10 headline functional;
- paper-facing quantitative welfare inequality.

Retain it as a theory-empirics compatibility finding. A bounded-support
or quantile analogue would be a new measure with a new name and is
deferred for discussion with François.


2. REVISED M08 MEASURE ROLES

W1.

W4.

Pre-registered fallback secondary if W4 fails and W6 passes:

validation only.

median, quantiles, minimum, maximum and weighted/unweighted counts
below, at and above zero. Do not report a conventional Gini or run a

transformation.

3. AUTHORISE ONE FINAL NESTED 8x BASIS
