# INDEX 04 - Responsibility and Equality of Opportunity v1

## Compensation Versus Responsibility

- **Claim:** The normative core is a compensation-responsibility distinction:
  some inequalities are compensation-relevant while others may be treated as
  responsibility-relevant.
  **Sources:** Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 JEL [explicit]; Decancq et al. 2015
  [explicit equivalent-income/fair-allocation lineage]; Bargain et al. 2013
  [explicit empirical responsibility metrics]; Jacquet et al. 2026 [explicit
  responsibility comparator]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** These sources do not all define the same responsibility
  boundary; use them as lineage and comparator support, not as identical models.

- **Claim:** In the JMP, responsibility sensitivity is operationalized twice:
  through the W^1-W^6 measure menu and through the access/ability/preference
  decomposition.
  **Sources:** JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit]; JMP project state v1 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** This dual operation is a JMP design statement, not a
  result from the T1A/T1B literature.

## Circumstances Versus Preferences

- **Claim:** A standard responsibility framing contrasts circumstances with
  preferences/effort, but the JMP refines this into access, ability, and
  preference.
  **Sources:** Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 JEL [explicit Roemer/circumstance
  framing]; Jacquet et al. 2026 [explicit preference/circumstance comparator];
  Capeau & Decoster 2016 [explicit two-way opportunity/preference predecessor];
  JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit three-way cut].
  **Do not overclaim:** Do not use two-way sources as if they already contain the
  three-way access/ability/preference decomposition.

- **Claim:** Preferences remain respected in the JMP welfare metric, even when
  preference is later treated as a source in the decomposition.
  **Sources:** JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit]; Decancq et al. 2015 [explicit];
  Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 JEL [explicit preference-respecting money metric];
  Bhattacharya 2015 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** Preference-respecting welfare is distinct from
  preference-neutralised comparators such as Jacquet et al.'s CVcirc or Aaberge &
  Colombino's common utility.

## Fair Income / Equivalent Income

- **Claim:** Equivalent income is the literature's bridge between heterogeneous
  preferences and comparable welfare levels.
  **Sources:** Decancq et al. 2015 [explicit]; Bargain et al. 2013 [explicit];
  Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 JEL [explicit money-metric definition]; Fleurbaey &
  Maniquet 2018 IJET [explicit axiomatisation].
  **Do not overclaim:** Equivalent-income lineage supports the welfare primitive,
  not the full W^1-W^6 taxonomy.

- **Claim:** Reference wages, reference labor, reference preferences, or
  reference sets encode the ethical stance of a money-metric measure.
  **Sources:** Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 JEL [explicit]; Bargain et al. 2013
  [explicit three metric formulas]; Jacquet et al. 2026 [explicit reference
  preference]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** Jacquet et al.'s reference-preference CV is a comparator,
  not the JMP's own preference-respecting level measure.

## Ability As Contested Channel

- **Claim:** Ability is contested because wage technology can be read either as
  a responsibility-relevant productivity channel or as a compensation-relevant
  circumstance, so the JMP reports access-only and access-plus-ability brackets.
  **Sources:** JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit]; Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 JEL
  [explicit compensate-skill vs own-skill axis]; Dagsvik & Jia 2016 [explicit
  individual-ability wage component, mapped by analogy]; Capeau & Decoster 2016
  [explicit wage-offer density, mapped by analogy].
  **Do not overclaim:** The JEL paper is silent on access and does not identify
  wage technology econometrically; RURO sources provide wage opportunity objects
  but not the full normative classification.

- **Claim:** Education-as-access is a named robustness cut, not the baseline.
  **Sources:** JMP project state v1 [explicit]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit];
  Beffy et al. 2019 [weak/analogical precedent because education shifts hours
  offer weights]; Jacquet et al. 2026 [weak/analogical because education shifts
  availability but ability is unmodeled].
  **Do not overclaim:** Beffy and Jacquet do not settle the JMP's ability/access
  boundary.

## Access As Non-Responsibility Channel

- **Claim:** Access is the opportunity side the household does not fully control:
  market participation, hours availability, region/year environment, and
  occupation availability in the baseline.
  **Sources:** JMP project state v1 [explicit]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit];
  Capeau & Decoster 2016 [explicit access through offer intensity and hours];
  Beffy et al. 2019 [explicit hours-access]; Dagsvik & Jia 2016 [explicit
  hours/market opportunity, mapped by analogy].
  **Do not overclaim:** Only the JMP project files support occupation as access;
  the accepted literature mostly omits occupation or treats it ambiguously.

- **Claim:** Access must remain separate from EUROMOD budget construction:
  EUROMOD prices each package, while access governs which packages are feasible.
  **Sources:** Sutherland & Figari 2013 [explicit infrastructure]; Capeau &
  Decoster 2016 [explicit EUROMOD budget map and separate opportunity density];
  JMP project state v1 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** EUROMOD is not the access channel and has no welfare or
  responsibility content.

## Relationship To The Separate Theory Paper

- **Claim:** The empirical JMP imports normative readings and the W^1-W^6 family
  as cited primitives from the companion theory paper; it does not reproduce the
  axioms or present itself as the theory contribution.
  **Sources:** JMP project state v1 [explicit]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit];
  QC report v1 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** Do not attribute companion-paper axioms to Fleurbaey &
  Maniquet 2018 JEL/IJET, Jacquet et al. 2026, Bargain et al. 2013, or any RURO
  source.

- **Claim:** The accepted literature supplies published anchors for the
  primitives around the theory boundary: money-metric utility,
  preference-respecting welfare, and responsibility/reference choice.
  **Sources:** Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 JEL [explicit]; Fleurbaey & Maniquet
  2018 IJET [explicit]; Decancq et al. 2015 [explicit]; Bargain et al. 2013
  [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** Published anchors are not a substitute for citing the
  companion theory paper when the text discusses W^1-W^6 characterisation.
