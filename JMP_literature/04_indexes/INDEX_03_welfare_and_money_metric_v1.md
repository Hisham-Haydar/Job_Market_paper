# INDEX 03 - Welfare and Money-Metric v1

## Random-Utility Welfare

- **Claim:** The JMP welfare layer is built downstream of a random-utility job
  choice model, but it converts attained utility to money rather than stopping
  at choice probabilities.
  **Sources:** Dagsvik & Karlstrom 2005 [explicit welfare apparatus for RUM];
  Bargain et al. 2013 [explicit empirical equivalent-income labor-supply
  welfare]; Jacquet et al. 2026 [explicit CV/CVcirc comparator]; JMP welfare
  spec v5 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** Bargain and Jacquet are comparators; neither contains the
  JMP's six-measure level welfare object.

- **Claim:** Welfare calculations must respect heterogeneous preferences rather
  than evaluate everyone through raw income alone.
  **Sources:** Bargain et al. 2013 [explicit]; Decancq et al. 2015 [explicit];
  Bhattacharya 2015 [explicit arbitrary preference heterogeneity]; Fleurbaey &
  Maniquet 2018 JEL [explicit]; Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 IJET [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** Preference-respecting welfare is not the same as the
  companion theory paper's W^1-W^6 classification.

## Inclusive Value

- **Claim:** The JMP baseline welfare object is ex ante: attained utility is the
  expected maximum / inclusive value over the feasible set.
  **Sources:** JMP project state v1 [explicit]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit];
  Dagsvik & Karlstrom 2005 [derived-by-analogy for analytic-in-shocks welfare
  machinery]; Jacquet et al. 2026 [background contrast]; Aaberge & Colombino
  2013 [background contrast because it explicitly declines the expected-maximum
  route].
  **Do not overclaim:** Dagsvik & Karlstrom supply CV/EV machinery, not the
  exact JMP level object. Aaberge & Colombino 2013 is a contrast, not support.

- **Claim:** Ex-post realised-job welfare is insufficient for the JMP's access
  question because it cannot value the feasible set itself.
  **Sources:** JMP project state v1 [explicit]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit];
  Capeau & Decoster 2016 [background contrast: ex-post utility simulation];
  Aaberge & Colombino 2013 [background contrast: ex-post simulated choice].
  **Do not overclaim:** The contrast does not mean ex-post objects are invalid
  generally; they are not the baseline for this opportunity paper.

## Compensating Variation / Equivalent Variation

- **Claim:** CV/EV in nonlinear-in-income random utility requires expenditure
  function or inversion machinery rather than the simple linear-in-income
  log-sum shortcut.
  **Sources:** Dagsvik & Karlstrom 2005 [explicit]; Jacquet et al. 2026
  [explicit CV application]; Bhattacharya 2015 [explicit EV/CV identification];
  Bhattacharya 2018 [background].
  **Do not overclaim:** CV/EV are secondary comparability/cross-check forms in
  the welfare spec, not the JMP's primary level welfare family.

- **Claim:** CV/CVcirc in Jacquet et al. is the nearest competitor because it
  combines latent jobs, money-metric welfare changes, and a responsibility
  contrast.
  **Sources:** Jacquet et al. 2026 [explicit]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit
  positioning].
  **Do not overclaim:** Jacquet et al. vary the preference/circumstance axis in
  a tax-reform change object; the JMP varies the access/pay responsibility
  treatment in a level welfare object.

## Money-Metric Welfare

- **Claim:** Money-metric/equivalent-income welfare is the appropriate language
  for comparing heterogeneous preferences because the measure is expressed in a
  common money unit while respecting each household's own indifference map.
  **Sources:** Bargain et al. 2013 [explicit empirical implementation]; Decancq
  et al. 2015 [explicit equivalent-income principle]; Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018
  JEL [explicit definition/reference-wage logic]; Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 IJET
  [explicit money-metric axiomatisation]; JMP project state v1 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** These sources ground the primitive; the exact six-measure
  taxonomy is not in them unless stated as a JMP/theory-paper import.

- **Claim:** Reference choice is not a technical nuisance; it is the normative
  lever that determines which dimensions are compensated or treated as
  responsibility.
  **Sources:** Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 JEL [explicit]; Bargain et al. 2013
  [explicit metric family on a responsibility-for-preferences axis]; Decancq et
  al. 2015 [background]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** The JEL paper gives a one-axis skill/reference-wage
  spectrum, not the JMP's two-axis access/pay menu.

## Heterogeneous Preferences

- **Claim:** Welfare can be preference-respecting even when the full
  heterogeneity distribution is not separately recovered nonparametrically.
  **Sources:** Bhattacharya 2015 [explicit]; Bhattacharya 2018 [background];
  Bargain et al. 2013 [explicit empirical preference heterogeneity]; Decancq et
  al. 2015 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** This is a welfare-identification claim, not a guarantee
  that the structural access/ability/preference channel split is identified.

- **Claim:** Preference-neutralised welfare objects are useful comparators but
  are not the JMP's primary preference-respecting object.
  **Sources:** Jacquet et al. 2026 [explicit CVcirc]; Aaberge & Colombino 2013
  [explicit common-utility contrast]; Bargain et al. 2013 [explicit metric
  family]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** Do not call CVcirc or Aaberge-Colombino common utility a
  member of W^1-W^6.

## Welfare Under Constrained Opportunities

- **Claim:** For an opportunity paper, welfare must be computed over the
  household's feasible set, because access changes attained utility under every
  measure.
  **Sources:** JMP project state v1 [explicit]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit];
  Dagsvik & Karlstrom 2005 [derived-by-analogy for welfare given a feasible set];
  Jacquet et al. 2026 [background constrained-choice CV].
  **Do not overclaim:** Dagsvik & Karlstrom do not model opportunity-density
  formation; they supply downstream welfare machinery.

- **Claim:** EUROMOD enters welfare only as the disposable-income map for each
  job package.
  **Sources:** Sutherland & Figari 2013 [explicit infrastructure]; Bargain et al.
  2013 [explicit EUROMOD welfare comparator]; Capeau & Decoster 2016 [explicit
  EUROMOD budget map]; JMP project state v1 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** EUROMOD is not a welfare theory, opportunity mechanism,
  or decomposition source.

## Relation To W^1-W^6

- **Claim:** The JMP computes a family W^1-W^6 spanning full responsibility,
  one-sided compensation, and full compensation; the family is imported from the
  companion theory paper and used empirically.
  **Sources:** JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit]; JMP project state v1 [explicit].
  **Do not overclaim:** Do not attribute this family to Bargain et al.,
  Fleurbaey-Maniquet JEL/IJET, Decancq et al., Bhattacharya, or any RURO paper.

- **Claim:** The accepted literature can support pieces of the W^1-W^6 design:
  preference-respecting money metric, reference-choice responsibility logic,
  random-utility inversion, and constrained feasible sets.
  **Sources:** Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 JEL [explicit money-metric/reference];
  Fleurbaey & Maniquet 2018 IJET [explicit money-metric axiomatisation];
  Bargain et al. 2013 [explicit empirical equivalent-income family]; Dagsvik &
  Karlstrom 2005 [explicit inversion/CV machinery]; JMP welfare spec v5
  [explicit synthesis].
  **Do not overclaim:** The synthesis is the JMP's design; the sources do not
  jointly characterise W^1-W^6.

## Overclaim Warnings

- **Claim:** No accepted source by itself establishes the JMP's complete welfare
  architecture.
  **Sources:** QC report v1 [explicit]; JMP welfare spec v5 [explicit]; each
  welfare summary section 13 [explicit warnings].
  **Do not overclaim:** Keep the paper's empirical JMP separate from the
  Haydar-Maniquet theory paper, and mark W^1-W^6 references as imported/cited
  primitives.
