sources. LaTeX `\cite{}` commands may now use all five DR03 canonical bibkeys.
Citation status for page numbers is not final for Bloemen 2000 until the
journal-article PDF is acquired and the MD extraction is updated. Any writing
that uses pending page-number sources should carry
`[DP version; journal pages TBC]` in draft notes.

2021, Crede/Grammatikos 2022, Sun-Leung 2019) remain gated and may not appear
in any bibliography entry until `JMP_DR03_verification_log_v1.md` records
