## Hold the line in Q&A

- **Primary measure.** EA is not the paper's primary measure. Prospects and attained outcomes answer different welfare questions, and the choice between them is not made here.
- **Access.** Access means local access, not total opportunity.
- **Status of the shares.** No share is causal, and none is a total share of inequality due to job opportunities.
