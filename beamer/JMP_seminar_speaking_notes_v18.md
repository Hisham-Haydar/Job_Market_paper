## Scripted sentences for slides 16 and B26

Use each sentence **only on its own slide**. The two slides use different denominators; never combine them.

- **Slide 16 (shares of baseline Gini):** "Under the current restricted ex-ante decomposition, labour-market opportunities are associated with about twenty per cent of baseline welfare inequality for single adults and about eight per cent for couples, on the main equivalised scale."
- **Backup B26, composition of the explained component (shares of the explained change):** "Within that currently explained component, most of the single-adult opportunity effect comes through access rather than earning opportunities."

On slide 16, point to two rows:

1. **The couples preference row.** It is negative. Access plus earnings alone is the headline couples number, but preferences plus access plus earnings is smaller, because the preference contribution is negative.
2. **The last row, "not allocated by this exercise".** It is **not a channel**, and it is not "needs explain the rest". The exercise holds household resources, needs and composition fixed. It also keeps the sex-specific blocks, and it does not equalise wage-draw luck or the common offer spread. Those are larger than the location differences that the access and earning operators remove.

## Hold the line

- **Causality.** No share is causal.
- **Primary measure.** EA is not the paper's primary measure; the choice between the two perspectives is open.
- **Access.** Access is *local* access, not total opportunity.
- **Elasticities.** No elasticities are reported: the current record states that wage elasticities are not reported. If asked, say so. Do not quote an old number.
